<p><strong>The gap between imagination and implementation has collapsed.</strong></p>
<p>Last year, when I started building <strong><a href="https://x.com/deepfashionhq">DeepFashion AI</a></strong>, AI could not replace a full team.<br>I still had to organize the codebase, design the product, and translate my taste into execution—manually.</p>
<p>One year later, I built <strong><a href="https://77z.tech">77z.tech</a></strong> in <strong>two days</strong>.</p>
<div><figure><a href="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/%24s_!0A1M!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa14c1070-b279-4c34-a366-a4bb84bb7fdb_1310x1011.png"><div><img src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/%24s_!0A1M!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa14c1070-b279-4c34-a366-a4bb84bb7fdb_1310x1011.png" width="1310" height="1011" alt=""></div>
</a></figure>
</div>
<p>Not mockups.<br>Not a demo.<br>The <em>entire</em> product website.</p>
<p>Copy. Design. Icons. CSS. Animations.<br>Five localized languages. Fully mobile-responsive.</p>
<p>AI wrote it all.</p>
<p>I’ve been building products(Hardware &amp; Software) for over 20 years. What used to require a team of five and three months now happens in a weekend.</p>
<p>But here’s the part most people misunderstand:</p>
<p><strong>AI didn’t replace the work, specially in software. It replaced the waiting.</strong></p>
<p>No waiting for design reviews.<br>No “can you move this 2px left?” across 47 Slack messages.<br>No context switching between Figma, VS Code, and Notion.</p>
<p>The thinking is still mine.<br>The vision is still mine.<br>The judgment about what matters is still mine.</p>
<p>AI simply executes—instantly, repeatedly, without friction.</p>
<h3>What this changes</h3>
<ul>
<li><p><strong>Speed</strong>: Days instead of months.</p>
</li>

<li><p><strong>Solo power</strong>: One person now equals a full product team.</p>
</li>

<li><p><strong>The new moat</strong>: Taste &gt; execution. Everyone can build; few know what’s worth building.</p>
</li>

<li><p><strong>Documentation becomes product</strong>: I wrote strategy docs. AI turned them into a website. Same brain, different output.</p>
</li>

</ul>
<p>This is not a productivity upgrade.</p>
<p>This is a <strong>capability explosion</strong>.</p>
<p>The real question is no longer:<br><em>“How do I use AI to work faster?”</em></p>
<p>It is:<br><strong>“What becomes possible when building is essentially free?”</strong></p>
