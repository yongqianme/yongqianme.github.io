<p>Over the past 20 years, I’ve operated at the intersection of physical AI, edge networking, and SaaS—building and exiting ventures spanning industrial connectivity, mesh VPNs, and AI-driven fashion design.</p>
<p>One thing I’ve learned is that 0-to-1 execution is rarely about high-level theory. It’s about solving concrete, messy technical and career challenges from scratch. </p>
<p>To help builders, systems engineers, and founders navigate these hurdles, I’ve packaged my boots-on-the-ground experience into four playbooks:</p>
<h2><strong>1. How to Build Fashion AI SaaS From Scratch</strong></h2>

<p>A practical guide drawing from my journey building and selling DeepFashion. It covers everything from training Stable Diffusion models on rented A100 GPUs and managing cloud GPU costs to leveraging AI to write 80% of the codebase.</p>
<p>Link: https://www.amazon.com/build-Fashion-Scratch-step-step-ebook/dp/B0F8WBNSPP</p>

<p>A hands-on systems engineering guide based on my experience scaling OmniEdge VPN. It walks through WireGuard integrations, zero-trust mesh network overlays, cloud orchestration, and cross-platform native client deployments.</p>
<p>Link: https://www.amazon.com/Building-Enterprise-Peer-Peer-Scratch-ebook/dp/B0F8YSG1WW</p>

<p>Transitioning from Founder/CEO back to an employee is one of the hardest career pivots. This book is a practical framework to help former founders counter recruiter bias, reframe their resumes from “doing everything” to “delivering key metrics,” and secure senior operator roles.</p>
