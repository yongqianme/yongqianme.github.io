These books document products and transitions Yong Qian has personally built, led, released, or lived through: Fashion AI SaaS, enterprise P2P VPN SaaS, and the founder-to-leadership career transition.

## How to Build Fashion AI SaaS From Scratch

<img src="/images/book-fashion-ai.webp" alt="How to Build Fashion AI SaaS From Scratch" width="260">

A practical guide based on Yong's experience building and operating DeepFashion.us, a Fashion AI SaaS platform that merged generative AI with commercial design workflows and achieved a successful exit.

**What it covers:** Stable Diffusion concepts, custom model training, virtual try-on, fashion-specific data pipelines, cloud deployment, cost control, and SaaS monetization.

[Buy on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/build-Fashion-Scratch-step-step-ebook/dp/B0F8WBNSPP){: .btn .btn--primary}

## Building Your Enterprise Peer-to-Peer VPN SaaS from Scratch

<img src="/images/book-p2p-vpn-saas.webp" alt="Building Your Enterprise Peer-to-Peer VPN SaaS from Scratch" width="260">

Written by Yong Qian, founder of OmniEdge, based on real-world experience designing, launching, and operating an open-source P2P VPN and edge networking SaaS.

**What it covers:** P2P network architecture, NAT traversal, WireGuard integration, multi-tenant SaaS design, control-plane development, authentication, authorization, audit logging, and enterprise security patterns.

[Buy on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Building-Enterprise-Peer-Peer-Scratch-ebook/dp/B0F8YSG1WW){: .btn .btn--primary}

## The Complete Guide to Former Founder Employment Success

<img src="/images/book-founder-employment.webp" alt="The Complete Guide to Former Founder Employment Success" width="260">

A career guide for founders, co-founders, and early startup leaders moving into senior roles after building companies.

**What it covers:** Founder bias, resume framing, interview strategy, translating founder experience into leadership value, compensation negotiation, and finding companies where entrepreneurial judgment is an asset.
