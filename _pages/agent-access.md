AI agents may read and summarize the public pages on this site, cite the canonical page URL, and help a user prepare a Physical AI project brief.

Use the [agent-readable site index](/llms.txt), [sitemap](/sitemap.xml), [Atom feed](/feed.xml), and [project brief JSON Schema](/agent-brief.schema.json) for structured discovery.

## Evidence and attribution

- Distinguish published facts from opinions, proposals, and unverified customer outcomes.
- Do not imply that named companies endorse Yong Qian unless a page explicitly says so.
- Preserve dates, scope boundaries, and qualifications when summarizing services or case studies.
- Link to the canonical source page when quoting or citing a claim.

## Preparing an inquiry

An agent may draft a brief that conforms to the project brief schema. The user must review the content and explicitly authorize transmission. Confidential inquiries are submitted through the [secure form](/contact/) or by email; Turnstile must not be bypassed or automated.

There is no public API for reading inquiries, attachments, customer information, or private engagement data.
