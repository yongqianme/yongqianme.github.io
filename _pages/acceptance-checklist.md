# Robotics acceptance checklist

Use this worksheet to agree acceptance before implementation. Adapt it to the specific process; it does not replace the site's engineering and safety procedures.

**Project / process:** ____________________  **Site:** ____________________

**Customer owner:** ____________________  **Technical lead:** ____________________

## Scope and baseline

- [ ] Define the covered assets, interfaces, exclusions, and operating conditions.
- [ ] Agree the observation period, product mix, and baseline evidence.
- [ ] Record downtime, fault, cycle-time, and quality measures as applicable.

**Baseline / target / units:** ___________________________________________________

## Test conditions and readiness

- [ ] Set test duration, sample size, measurement method, and allowed interruptions.
- [ ] Confirm site authorization, engineering and safety review, backups, and rollback.
- [ ] Assign customer, specialist, and supplier responsibilities.

**Test window / evidence owner:** _______________________________________________

## Evidence and decision

- [ ] Compare results under representative, agreed conditions.
- [ ] Check for adverse effects on quality, recovery time, or adjacent processes.
- [ ] List unresolved issues and agree whether to accept, retest, or revise scope.

**Evidence location / open issues:** ______________________________________________

## Handover

- [ ] Deliver operating and recovery procedures; complete operator handover.
- [ ] Agree support hours, covered assets, escalation contact, and remaining actions.

**Decision / date:** ____________________  **Customer sign-off:** ____________________
